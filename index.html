High protein (~2 g/kg) is the key to losing fat while keeping & building muscle. Hit protein first every day; carbs and fat can flex around it.

How this is worked out: BMR (Mifflin–St Jeor) ≈ 1,835 kcal → with 3–4 gym sessions/week your maintenance (TDEE) ≈ 2,750 kcal. A ~450 kcal deficit → ~2,300 kcal/day, which loses fat steadily (~0.45 kg/week) so 5 kg comes off in roughly 10–12 weeks without crashing your strength.

Estimate assumes male, age 18–29. If that's off, tell me and I'll recompute — for female subtract ~160 kcal (≈ 2,150/day).

Cardio & steps

2–3× zone-2 cardio (incline walk or bike), 20–30 min — after lifting or on a rest day. Aim 8,000–10,000 steps/day. This is what makes the 5 kg come off without eating less.

Progression

Each session try to beat last time: add a rep, and once you hit the top of the range on all sets, add a little weight (double progression). Deload (lighter week) every 6–8 weeks.

Make it work

Weigh-in: same morning each week, after the toilet, before food. Judge the trend over 2–3 weeks, not day to day (water/food weight swings 1–2 kg).

If the scale stalls 2+ weeks: drop ~150 kcal (e.g. skip one optional snack) or add 1,500 steps/day. Keep protein high.

Meal prep: batch-cook rice, quinoa, lentils and roast a tray of veg on Sunday — Day A/B/C come together in minutes.

Eating out / busy day: prioritise a protein source (eggs, paneer, beans, Greek yogurt, a shake) and a vegetable; let the rest slide.

Hydration & sleep: 2.5–3 L water and 7–9 h sleep do more for recovery and appetite than any supplement.

This is general guidance, not medical or dietitian advice. If you have any health conditions or take medication, check with a doctor or registered dietitian before starting — especially before big calorie changes.
